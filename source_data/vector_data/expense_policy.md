# Expense Policy

Owner: People Ops.

## What can be expensed

Reasonable business expenses are reimbursed: travel, client meals, conference tickets and small equipment. Software subscriptions must be approved by the team lead before purchase.

## Limits

Meals while travelling are capped at 60 EUR per day. Flights over 500 EUR need approval from your manager before booking. Hotel stays are capped at 180 EUR per night in major cities.

## How to submit

Submit receipts within 30 days of the expense using the expense portal. Reports older than 30 days may be rejected. Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly payroll run.

## Home office

New employees have a one-time home office budget of 400 EUR, usable in the first 90 days. Larger purchases need a manager's approval.
