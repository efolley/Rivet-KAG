# Employee Onboarding Guide

Owner: People Ops. Applies to all new hires at Northwind Analytics (a fictional company used for this demo).

## Day one

New employees receive their laptop on day one. People Ops sends a welcome email the Friday before the start date with the schedule. Your manager runs a 30-minute kickoff and introduces you to your team.

## Accounts and access

Accounts are created through Okta single sign-on. You get email, Slack and GitHub access on day one. VPN access is only enabled after you complete the mandatory security training, usually within the first week. See the VPN and Access Policy for details.

## First 30 days

Week one is training and setup. By day 30 you should have shipped one small change to production or completed one deliverable agreed with your manager. A People Partner checks in with you at day 7, day 30 and day 90.

## Onboarding buddy

Every new hire gets a buddy from a different team. The buddy answers day-to-day questions and helps you learn how the company works. The onboarding process was redesigned in the Harbor project, which finished in 2025.
